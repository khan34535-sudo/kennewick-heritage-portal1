## ⚠ Disclaimer
This is a design prototype and demonstrator. It is intended to show how archaeological and tribal historical information could be presented respectfully, not to serve as a primary research source or a substitute for tribal consultation.

* Site names and APA citations point to real literature
* Coordinates, artifact descriptions, and numerical indices are illustrative examples
* All 3D models and artifact images are placeholders or generic anatomical/artifact references
* **Do not cite this prototype in academic work** — consult original publications and the claimant tribes

---

## What This Demonstrates
* **Spatial context** — Real satellite imagery with 3D terrain, hosting sites as spatial anchors along the Columbia River and institutional repositories.
* **Living Land Acknowledgement** — Foregrounding the sovereignty, oral histories, and reburial by the five claimant tribes (Colville, Nez Perce, Umatilla, Yakama, Wanapum).
* **3D specimen & artifact models** — Procedural Three.js models of skulls, spearpoints, DNA helices, and ceremonial bundles.
* **Multi-proxy integration** — Radiocarbon dating, bioarchaeology, genetics, ritual objects, and legal histories side-by-side.
* **Resilience narratives** — Explorable scenarios for scientific tension vs. Indigenous sovereignty, legal battles, and repatriation.
* **Readable citations** — Every panel surfaces its own APA 7th reference.
